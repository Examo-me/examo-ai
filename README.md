# Examo AI

Examo is a study workspace that turns your own course materials into structured summaries, flashcards, mind maps, and exam-style practice. Materials and study tools stay together by week or section, so you can work through a course at your own pace.

## Study with your own materials

1. [Open Examo](https://examo.ai/) and create a course.
2. Add lecture slides, notes, readings, or recordings and organize them into sections.
3. Review a summary, test yourself with practice questions, or ask [Loki, the course tutor](https://examo.ai/tools), to explain an idea from your materials.
